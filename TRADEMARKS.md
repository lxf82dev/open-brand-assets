# Brand assets and rights

Company and product names, logos, and trademarks belong to their respective owners. Their inclusion identifies the brand and does not imply affiliation, sponsorship, endorsement, or a partnership.

The project MIT license covers our original code, documentation, and skill. It does **not** relicense the images under `brands/`, grant trademark rights, or establish permission for any particular use. `LicenseRef-Brand-Owner` is a local identifier for retained owner rights, not an SPDX open-source license or a grant of permission.

An official download or public website icon establishes provenance, not a general redistribution grant. Each manifest records a usage status, checked date, source URLs, and notes. Some entries remain unverified or require permission. A full brand-policy review or legal clearance is outside what those records establish. Consult the linked official terms for your intended use and redistribution.

Upstream collections retain their own licenses and attribution requirements. The search tool links to their resources; it does not copy or relicense those collections. If contributors import upstream artwork, include its original notices and applicable license in the brand folder and identify that source in the manifest.

For corrections or removal, open an issue with the brand, asset path, official source, and requested change. Rights holders can use the repository owner's [GitHub profile](https://github.com/lxf82dev) for publicly listed contact information if the request should not be posted publicly. Do not post confidential agreements or personal contact information in an issue.
